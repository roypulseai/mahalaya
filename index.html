What is Mahalaya?

Mahalaya is the new-moon morning that opens the Durga Puja season. Families in Bengal wake before dawn to listen to the Mahishasuramardini, the telling of how Goddess Durga defeated the buffalo demon Mahishasura, with Chandipath recitation and devotional music. It is also the day for offering tarpan to ancestors. The Puja itself begins about a week later.
